Project Radix

Project Radix is the name I build and publish my own work under. Radix is Latin for root: every project starts small and grows from there.

When you see "Project Radix" at the bottom of a website or in a game, it means I made it.

About

I like turning messy, manual work into simple tools people actually use: a website that brings in customers, a dashboard that shows what's owed, a form that never loses a request.

I work across the whole thing, from the look and the words to the database behind it, and I keep things plain, fast and easy to change later.
